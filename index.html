About

I’m a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science Engineering at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University (SRMU), in India, graduating in 2029. Most of what I know so far comes from coursework and from trying things out on my own.

I started with C and Python, and I’m slowly adding HTML and CSS so I can make things people can actually open and use. I’m still building my fundamentals, and I’d rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise.

Outside of code, I like writing. I’ve been exploring content writing and copywriting alongside programming, because I enjoy explaining things clearly as much as I enjoy making them work.

Away from the screen, I play guitar. I was the lead guitarist in my school band for two to three years and played at school events and cultural fests, which is where I got over my stage fright and learned to work closely with a team.

I’m aiming to become a software developer, and I’m open to small projects and freelance work as I learn.
